A Voice For Your Wedding

Timeless • Elegant • Personal

Close your eyes for a moment and imagine… A day in your life you'll remember forever. You stand facing each other — your hearts racing, and perhaps a tear of joy gently rolls down your cheek. Love, captured in time, surrounded by the joy and prayers of your loved ones.

Your only word is "Yes",

while everything else will be expressed through my voice and music.

I'm Petra Golubovic Jez, a passionate singer with a deep love for creating heartfelt and timeless performances. I find joy in connecting with people through music and crafting moments that stay in the heart forever.

With years of experience in live performance and training in modern vocal technique, I bring a unique blend of vocal mastery and emotional depth to every song I perform.

Music has been my lifelong companion — from my early piano days to discovering that singing is my true calling. After completing my Bachelor's degree at the Academy of Music in Bosnia, I've sung with numerous ensembles and performed across half the world — from New York to the Sydney Opera House. These life experiences taught me to connect deeply with my emotions, something that shines through in every interpretation.

But the most important experience of my life is that, besides music, I have found true love and continue to live it every day. I carry it in my voice and use it to shape every note of your celebration, creating a moment to be cherished forever.
